How to Play

Step 1 – Choose a Target Select one of the three categories: 100, 500, or 1000.

Step 2 – View the Numbers A set of 9 numbers will appear on the screen.

Step 3 – Find the Combination Look for any 4 numbers that add up exactly to the target number.

Step 4 – Drag the Numbers Drag your chosen four numbers into the four empty boxes.

Step 5 – Check the Sum The game automatically calculates the total. Try different combinations until you reach the exact target.

Step 6 – Complete the Challenge When the sum matches the target, you successfully complete the challenge! 🎉

Step 7 – Next Challenge Click NEXT CHALLENGE to receive a new set of numbers and continue playing.
